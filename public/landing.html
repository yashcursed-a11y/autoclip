AUTOCLIPIN · Local Architecture & Creator Protections

Privacy Policy & Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)

Last updated: November 2026. AUTOCLIPIN operates with an uncompromised Local-First Privacy Architecture.

Core Architectural Guarantee: We do NOT operate remote media ingestion servers. Your footage, raw videos, trimmed segments, audio tracks, and exported reels stay 100% on your local machine.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No video content, audio files, or generated transcripts are uploaded to AUTOCLIPIN servers.
  • No session recording or mouse-movement trackers (we block all session replays including Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, FullStory, and Inspectlet).
  • No microphone, webcam, or ambient background data access.
  • No personal contact books or file system scanning outside your designated export folders.

2. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and international GDPR standards, you hold full legal rights:

  • Right to Erasure: One-click option in Studio Settings to permanently purge all cached waveforms, frame thumbnails, and job history.
  • Right to Data Portability: All project timelines, transcripts, and metadata are saved in standard open JSON/MP4 formats on your local disk.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke internet connectivity for the desktop application at any time; offline editing and local GPU clipping continue without restriction.
