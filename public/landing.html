Privacy Policy & DPDP Compliance

AUTOCLIPIN is engineered with a strict Local-First Architecture in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and global privacy standards.

  • 100% Local Video & Audio Processing: Your source video files, exported clips, and generated audio remain exclusively on your local disk (D:\AutoClip_Data). No video or voice recordings are uploaded to our servers.
  • Zero Tracking & Session Replay: We do NOT employ intrusive session recording (no Hotjar, Clarity, or FullStory) or third-party advertising tracking.
  • Right to Erasure & Data Export: You can export or permanently delete your jobs, clips, and license data at any time via Desktop Studio Settings with 1 click.
