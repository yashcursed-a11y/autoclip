1. Campaign Overview & Objective

AUTOCLIPIN is an autonomous AI video clipping and editing desktop suite for Windows. It lets streamers, podcasters, editors, and clipping channels take long streams or video files and turn them into viral 9:16 vertical shorts in seconds with neural face tracking, kinetic captions, and automatic moment detection.

The Goal: Hiring talented AI video editors, motion designers, and short-form creators from the Higgsfield community to produce high-retention, scroll-stopping promotional reels that showcase what AUTOCLIPIN can do and drive app downloads.

2. Deliverables & Technical Specifications

Video Format & Quality
  • Aspect Ratio: 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920)
  • Duration: 25 to 45 seconds (Fast, high-retention pacing)
  • Frame Rate: 30 FPS or 60 FPS
  • Export: MP4 (H.264/AAC), crisp bitrate (>15 Mbps)
Editing & Style Elements
  • Hook (0-3s): Visual pattern interrupt or meme hook

  • Sound Design: Energetic whooshes, pop hits, trending BGM

3. Core Features to Highlight

  • Smart Neural Face Tracking: Automatically detects the speaker and crops widescreen 16:9 videos into dynamic 9:16 vertical frames, keeping faces centered with smooth tracking.
  • One-Click Full AI Auto-Edit: Ingests full videos or YouTube links, finds the top viral clips, trims dead air, generates kinetic subtitles, and exports them automatically.
  • 100% Offline Standalone Processing: Complete privacy. All video rendering, face tracking, and speech models run directly on your PC with zero cloud wait-times.
  • Generous Free Tier: 70 reels free every single week, with Creator Max offering 50 reels/day (~1,500 reels/month).

7. Dos and Don'ts

DO (Best Practices)
  • Keep cuts fast (cut every 1.5 - 2.5 seconds).
  • Use trending SFX (pops, swooshes, subtle riser on transitions).
  • Ensure 100% SFW visual assets and audio.
  • Highlight the free download availability (70 reels/week).
DON'T (Strictly Avoid)
  • No slow tutorials or monotone step-by-step reading.
  • No robotic unpolished text-to-speech with zero emotion.
  • No low resolution or blurry visuals.
  • No outdated 16:9 letterboxed footage without vertical reframing.
